# UniShop 

> A full-stack campus marketplace that enables university students to buy, sell, and connect with other students.

**🚧 This project is under active development.**

UniShop is a university-focused marketplace designed to make buying and selling within a campus community simple, convenient, and trustworthy.

Students can create listings, discover items through search and filtering, view detailed item and seller information, and communicate directly with sellers through real-time chat to arrange a deal.

##  Problem

University students frequently need to buy or sell items such as:

- Subject Notes
- Semester papers
- Lab essentials
- Smartphones
- Laptops
- Books
- Bicycles
- Electronics
- Furniture
- Gaming equipment
- Other campus-related items

General-purpose marketplaces are not specifically designed around university communities.

UniShop aims to provide a dedicated marketplace where students can discover items from their campus community and communicate directly with sellers.

---

##  Solution

UniShop provides a centralized marketplace where students can:

- Create and manage item listings
- Discover items through search
- Filter and sort listings
- View detailed item information
- View seller information
- Save items to a wishlist (In progress)
- Contact sellers
- Chat with sellers in real time
- Arrange deals directly with other students
